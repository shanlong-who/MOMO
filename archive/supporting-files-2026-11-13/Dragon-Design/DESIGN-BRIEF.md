# Dragon design review

Status: visual and motion proposal for review. Generated with the built-in
image generation tool. This proposal has not been applied to either program.

## Visual direction

Enlarge the face and eyes while keeping a refined adult expression. Simplify
the long white hair into a few broad locks. Keep two distinctive crescent
horns, compact lavender dragon wings and a thick crescent-shaped white tail.
Use a simple ankle-length ivory dress with one lavender hem band and one
large loop-knot clasp. Remove small scales, lace, chains, repeated ornaments
and fine embroidery. Separate white, lavender and deep-violet color masses
with clean, readable outlines.

## Performance direction

Personality: a calm, warm and quietly playful dragon woman companion.
Use restrained human gestures: a slight nod, a small open-hand greeting,
a thoughtful fingertip at the chin, a brief head inclination and relaxed
shoulder movement. Wings and tail follow emotion with small delayed motion.
Keep waiting expectant and review attentive. A failed task should produce a
short concerned expression and recovery rather than a prolonged sad loop.
Movement can use a short graceful glide supported by subtle wing motion.
Reserve a brief celebratory lift for explicit interaction. Reduce random
walking and frequent action changes in the offline behavior.

## Concept prompt

Create a landscape design-review sheet showing three identical adult anime
white dragon women at the same scale and baseline: calm idle, restrained
open-hand greeting, thoughtful fingertip at chin. Ground every pose in the
supplied original reference and the first canonical base. Target a small
desktop companion with enlarged expressive face, elegant miniature adult
proportions, simplified broad white hair locks, bold ivory crescent horns,
violet eyes, simple ivory dress and lavender hem, one large loop-knot clasp,
compact lavender wings and thick white crescent tail. Use clean tapered
deep-purple outlines and simple opaque cel shading. Preserve warm adult
older-sister presence. No infant features, animal paws, crouching, hopping,
running, dog-tail wagging, effects, particles, text, scenery or shadows.
Use a plain pale warm gray background. This is a concept sheet, not an atlas.
